# Jumonkey

**A fun, interactive jungle adventure that teaches kids how to make smarter choices about electronics and electronic waste.**

Jumonkey is an educational browser game designed for elementary school students. Players explore a colorful jungle, make everyday decisions about electronics, earn bananas, and discover how their choices can help protect the environment.

Our goal is to make learning about electronic waste (e-waste) simple, engaging, and accessible through storytelling, interactive gameplay, and visual learning.

## Play the Game

**[Play Jumonkey](https://jumonkey.vercel.app/)**

## Features

### Interactive Jungle Adventure
- Explore a jungle-themed environment with a friendly monkey character.
- Complete six scenarios involving everyday electronics.
- Choose between different actions and learn from each decision.
- Earn bananas based on environmentally responsible choices.

### Personalized Results Dashboard
After completing the adventure, players can explore:
- **Jungle Score:** See how many bananas they earned.
- **Jungle Future:** Discover a visual representation of their jungle's health.
- **Your Choices:** Review their decisions and learn how to improve.
- **What You Learned:** Explore the four Rs: Reduce, Reuse, Repair, and Recycle.

### Interactive E-Waste Fun Facts
Four activities introduce environmental concepts through animations and visual comparisons:

1. **The Giant E-Waste Train:** Discover how much electronic waste the world generates.
2. **The Growing Gadget Pile:** Compare global e-waste in 2022 with projections for 2030.
3. **The Recycling Mystery:** Discover how much e-waste is formally collected and recycled.
4. **Hidden Treasure:** Learn about valuable materials inside old electronics.

Each activity includes interactive visuals, simple educational explanations, and supporting research sources.

### Read-to-Me Narration
- Natural-sounding prerecorded narration.
- Accessible audio playback throughout the dashboard.
- Designed to help younger children follow along.
- Audio can be started, stopped, or replayed.

### Jungle Promise
Players finish by choosing an environmentally friendly habit they want to practice.

An animated banana tree celebrates their promise and encourages them to make positive choices in everyday life.

### Kid-Friendly Design
- Retro-inspired pixel-art visuals.
- Large, easy-to-use buttons.
- Touch and keyboard controls.
- Visual feedback and animations.
- Responsive layouts.
- Jungle-themed replay transition.

## How to Play

1. Click **Start Adventure** and enter your name.
2. Move through the jungle using the arrow keys or on-screen controls.
3. Complete six scenarios by choosing what you would do.
4. Earn bananas and see how your decisions affect your results.
5. Explore the interactive dashboard and learn e-waste facts.
6. Make your Jungle Promise.
7. Click **Play Again** to begin another adventure.


Visit:

## Educational Focus

Jumonkey introduces four key sustainability principles:

| Principle | What It Means |
|---|---|
| **Reduce** | Avoid buying new electronics when you don't need them. |
| **Reuse** | Keep using or share devices that still work. |
| **Repair** | Ask a trusted adult about fixing devices instead of immediately replacing them. |
| **Recycle** | Work with an adult to recycle unwanted electronics safely. |

The game encourages children to think about their everyday choices and understand that small actions can contribute to a healthier planet.

## Research and Sources

The educational activities are informed by research on electronic waste, recycling, and responsible electronics consumption.

Key facts featured in the game include:

- The world generated approximately **62 million tonnes of e-waste in 2022**.
- Approximately **22.3%** of global e-waste was documented as formally collected and recycled in 2022.
- Global e-waste generation is projected to reach approximately **82 million tonnes by 2030**.
- Electronics contain recoverable materials, including copper, silver, and gold.

**References:**
- [Global E-waste Monitor 2024](https://ewastemonitor.info/the-global-e-waste-monitor-2024/)
- [World Health Organization — Electronic Waste](https://www.who.int/news-room/fact-sheets/detail/electronic-waste-(e-waste))
- [U.S. Environmental Protection Agency — Electronics Donation and Recycling](https://www.epa.gov/recycle/electronics-donation-and-recycling)

Detailed sources and explanations are also available through the game's **For Grown-Ups** sections.
